Notes & Methodology

This report forecasts full-year 2026 revenue for accounts acquired in 2026, laid out by acquisition cohort. Every row is a 2026 cohort = the month of the Master Account's First Finance Transaction (FFT); every column is a calendar month. Revenue starts on the diagonal (the cohort's FFT month) and matures to the right. Jan-Jul 2026 = actuals; Aug-Dec 2026 = forecast. The Aug-Dec cohort rows are new cohorts not yet acquired (fully forecast). The Historicals section repeats the exact layout for the 2025 cohorts (all actual).

2. Filters (all multi-select — untick to narrow; use the "all / none" links; Reset restores everything)

  • Region — MA billing-country geo region (APAC, EMEA, Greater China, Latam, N. America).
  • Demand Channel — acquisition demand group, 7 values (AnP, Digital Marketing, Enterprise, Other Marketing, Paid Marketing, Resellers, Sales Outbound). Resellers is derived by the reseller override, matching the business's 7-channel view.
  • Closing Motion — how the account was closed (OB Team, Resellers, Sales, Self-Served).
  • FFT Month (cohort) — which cohort rows are shown. Tick Jan+Feb and only the Jan & Feb cohorts appear everywhere; all totals/KPIs then cover just those cohorts.
  • Revenue Month — which calendar columns are shown. Tick Feb+Sep and you see Feb actuals & Sep forecast; totals re-sum to those months.

The two month filters are display / aggregation masks only — they change what is shown and summed, never the underlying forecast values.

3. Measures (what each number means)

  • Actual — booked revenue (Jan-Jul 2026 cells, green).
  • Forecast — projected revenue (Aug-Dec 2026 cells, hatched blue).
  • Full-year / Total — Actual + Forecast.
  • Month total — a calendar month's revenue summed across the shown cohorts. Cohort total — a cohort's revenue across the shown months.
  • YoY Growth % — this month's 2026 total ÷ the same month's 2025 total − 1 (green up, red down). The Cohort-total cell = full-year 2026 ÷ full-year 2025 − 1.
  • Target — the 2026 Acquisition-plan revenue for that month / segment.
  • AvT % (Actual-vs-Target) — value ÷ Target × 100. Green ≥100%, amber 90-99%, red <90%.
  • vs plan (Aug-Dec breakdown) — Forecast ÷ Aug-Dec Target − 1 (signed; how far the forecast sits above/below plan).
  • Full-year AvT % (breakdown 2026 section) — full-year actual + forecast ÷ full-year target (traffic-light: green ≥100%, amber 90-99%, red <90%).
  • Growth factor (Mj → Mj+1) — how a cohort's monthly revenue changes from one age to the next; >1 rising, <1 tapering.

4. Sections in this report

  • Cohort revenue triangle — the 2026 cohort × month matrix, with Month total, YoY Growth %, Target and AvT % rows beneath.
  • Monthly revenue chart — actual vs forecast per selected calendar month.
  • Growth factors & curve — the maturation factors used for the selection vs the all-segments (global) curve. (Not affected by the month filters.)
  • Historicals (2025) — the same triangle for 2025 cohorts (all actual; no target/YoY as 2024 isn't in scope).
  • Forecast vs target breakdown — by Region / Demand / Closing, in three sections (Jan-Jul, Aug-Dec, Full-year), each showing the value + Target and %.
  • Demand × Closing and Region × Closing crosstabs (2026) — each cell shows total, actual + forecast, and AvT% | YoY%. Both always use the Actual + Forecast basis: AvT = total (actual + forecast) ÷ full-year target; YoY = total ÷ full 2025 (over the selected months).
  • 2025 actuals - Demand × Closing crosstab — last-year actual revenue per demand channel × closing motion (2025 has no forecast or target, and no YoY as 2024 is out of scope). Responds to all filters and the Demand view toggle.
  • Demand view (Demand × Closing crosstabs only) — Granular shows the seven demand channels as-is; Consolidated merges the demand rows: Other Marketing → Digital Marketing and Resellers → AnP. Revenue, target and 2025 all roll up together so AvT% and YoY% stay correct.

5. Forecast methodology — recency-weighted SUMPRODUCT chain-ladder, bottom-up

  1. Growth factors per finest segment (Region × Demand × Closing): age-to-age factor = SUMPRODUCT(weight, rev@age j+1) ÷ SUMPRODUCT(weight, rev@age j) over all cohorts Jan 2025 → Jul 2026 (complete months), with weight = 0.85^(cohort recency rank) so the newest cohorts count most.
  2. Credibility blend for thin segments: factor = w · own + (1-w) · global, w = min(1, base ÷ $150,000). Only the growth shape is borrowed - each segment's level always comes from its own data.
  3. Projection: existing cohorts (Jan-Jul) grow from their July actual by chaining the factors; new cohorts (Aug-Dec) start at the average Jan-Jul first-month revenue and grow by the cumulative curve.
  4. Bottom-up, no pooling: every finest segment is forecast on its own factors and then summed, so any region / channel / motion subtotal adds up exactly to the total. No top-line reconciliation is applied.

6. Targets, AvT caveats & limitations

  • Targets are calendar-month with no cohort dimension. With an FFT-Month subset, AvT compares a cohort-subset's revenue to the full monthly plan (reads partial-vs-full); at FFT Month = all it is exact. The Revenue-Month filter subsets targets cleanly.
  • Demand channel now includes Resellers as a 7th channel (reseller override), matching the plan's own taxonomy - so demand-level AvT is like-for-like against the plan. Region, Demand Channel, Closing Motion and totals reconcile to the ~$83.1M 2026 plan (Resellers target ~$9.9M).
  • Assumes 2026 cohorts mature like 2025; macro, pricing and mix shifts are not modelled. Jul 2026 is treated as final (may still settle slightly). Revenue is View_Fact_Revenue (~85% of reported), not the 100% FDI/Accounting model.
